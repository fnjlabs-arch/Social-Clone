! Title: Social Clone — TikTok app-upsell hiders
! Description: Best-effort cosmetic rules that hide TikTok web's "Open app"
!              upsells (the persistent top pill and app banners) inside
!              Social Clone. TikTok rotates its markup regularly, so these
!              rules are expected to need occasional maintenance.
! Expires: 30 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/fnjlabs-arch/Social-Clone
!
! The persistent "Open app" pill in TikTok's top bar. These target the
! leaf button/link itself (its exact label), so they cannot hide page
! content by accident.
tiktok.com##button:has-text(/^Open app$/)
tiktok.com##a:has-text(/^Open app$/)
tiktok.com##button:has-text(/^Open TikTok$/)
!
! App-download banner containers, when TikTok uses these class names.
! Inert when the classes are absent.
tiktok.com##[class*="AppBanner"]
tiktok.com##[class*="app-banner"]
tiktok.com##[class*="DownloadBanner"]
